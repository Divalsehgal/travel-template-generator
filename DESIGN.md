# Design System: Travel Itinerary Templates

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The template system caters to diverse traveler personas, from minimalists to tech enthusiasts.

## 2. Template Styles
* **Modern**: Clean lines, high-contrast typography, and generous whitespace. Focuses on clarity and "Premium Minimal" aesthetic.
* **Wavy**: Fluid sections, organic shapes, and soft gradients. Evokes a sense of adventure and natural flow.
* **Techno**: Monospaced accents, grid-based layouts, and sharp dividers. Brutalist-inspired but refined for "Industrial Utilitarian" vibe.
* **Futuristic**: Neon accents, glassmorphic cards, and dynamic depth. High-tech "Cyberpunk Refined" look.

## 3. Color Palette Roles
* **Primary**: Main brand color (usually Deep Blue or Teal).
* **Accent**: High-visibility color for calls to action or "D1" markers.
* **Neutral**: Grays for background depth and secondary text.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons**: Pill-shaped for Modern/Wavy, Sharp for Techno/Futuristic.
* **Stats**: Circular icons for Modern/Wavy, Boxed for Techno.

## 5. Layout Principles
All templates follow the A4 ratio (210mm x 297mm) to ensure perfect PDF generation.
